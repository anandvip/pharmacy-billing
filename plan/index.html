1. Goal and release target for v2

v2 should be a pharmacy counter app a pharmacist is proud to open. It should be easy to learn in ten minutes, make a bill in four taps, and never lose or double a single rupee. It stays a free, single web app that works offline on the phone with no server, carries the shop’s own logo and colour on every bill, and keeps the books safe with automatic backup nudges and a checked restore. The target is one complete release, built in small tested steps on the preview, and switched live only after you’ve tried every flow on your phone and said go.

New bill (new customer added in place)
  1. Home › New bill (1 tap) Opens the one-screen bill from Sample A.
  2. Customer (1 tap) Type 2 or 3 letters and tap the name, or tap Walk-in. For someone new, tap + Add ‘Ramesh’ (example name); the phone number is optional and the cart stays.
  3. Medicines (1 tap each) Search by name or salt and tap the medicine. Use + and − for strips or loose tablets. Low stock and expiry show beside each one.
  4. Payment (0 to 1 tap) Cash is already picked. Tap UPI, Part or Credit if needed. Walk-ins can’t go on credit.
  5. Save and share (1 tap) The button locks until the save finishes, then the bill image opens for WhatsApp. Changing the customer never empties the cart.
Take payment
  1. Home › Take payment (1) Or Take payment from inside a customer’s khata.
  2. Pick the customer (1) Their pending amount shows at once.
  3. Amount The full pending is filled in; edit it for a part payment. ‘Pending after’ updates as you type.
  4. Cash or UPI (0 to 1), then Save (1) Locks against a double tap. You can share a receipt image.
Who owes me
  1. Home › Who owes me (1) The total to collect, and customers sorted by biggest due, with how many days since they last paid.
  2. Tap a customer (1) Their khata: bills, payments and returns with a running balance, plus refills due.
  3. Actions Take payment, send a WhatsApp reminder with the statement image, or make a month bill.
Add or edit medicine
  1. Home › Add medicine (1) Name, strength, pack (for example a strip of 10), MRP, stock and expiry. Salt and batch are optional.
  2. Save (1) The same duplicate check as today: no two medicines with the same name and strength.
  3. Edit More › Stock list › search › tap the medicine › change › Save. Price changes go in the audit trail.
Return or void
  1. Open the bill (1) From Today’s bills or the customer’s khata.
  2. Return packs (1) › PIN › quantities › Save return (1) Stock goes back on the shelf. The refund is at the price actually charged.
  3. Or Void (1) › PIN › reason › Confirm (1) The bill stays in the list, crossed out, with the reason. Its stock goes back, and any money taken with it is marked void. Nothing is ever deleted.
Today’s bills and Reports
  1. Home › Today’s bills (1) Number, time, customer, amount, and paid or credit. Tap one to share it again, return, void or edit. An edit keeps the old version.
  2. More › Reports (2) Today, Month or Financial year: sales, money collected, credit given, returns, discounts, top medicines, low stock and expiring soon. Download as Excel (CSV) or PDF.

7. Branding and personalisation without a server

How the logo is kept
Resized on the phone to 256 px on the longest side and saved as a compressed image inside the shop’s settings in the phone’s own storage (IndexedDB). Today’s app already does this at 200 px.
Size cap
100 KB after compression. A bigger picture is shrunk automatically, and only refused if it still won’t fit.
Where it shows
The app’s top bar, the bill view, the PDF, and the WhatsApp bill image (drawn on the phone, as today’s drawDoc does)
Backups
Inside every backup file, so a new phone gets the logo back on restore

What can and can’t be personalised

ItemPer shop?Why
Logo, name, address, colours, footer on billsYesAll drawn on the phone from the saved settings
App’s top bar and welcomeYesSame
Browser tab titleYesSet when the app opens
Home-screen icon and app nameNoThe phone reads these from a fixed install file (the manifest) on the website. A per-shop manifest made on the phone is unreliable: Android may refuse to install it or ignore it, and iPhones read the icon only once, when the app is added.
  1. Recommended for now: one good generic app icon with the new app name. The shop’s logo shows the moment the app opens.
  2. Later, for a shop that wants its own icon: a small static folder on the same web address with its own icon and name. Same web address, so the books stay the same.
  3. Much later: a Play Store wrapper app. Deferred.

8. Data reliability

The most important section. A billing app nobody trusts is a billing app nobody uses. Without a server, the books live on one phone, so v2 has to make losing them very hard and backing them up nearly effortless.

What can wipe the books

RiskWhat happensv2 protection
Phone low on spaceThe browser may clear the site’s storageAsk the phone to keep the data (below). The backup nudge
Clear browsing data or site dataEverything for this web address is goneA warning in Help. Backups
iPhone, used in Safari and not installedSafari deletes site storage after 7 days of use without opening the siteInstall to the home screen first, which is exempt. The welcome card asks for it
Lost, broken or changed phoneThe books are on that phone onlyBackups sent off the phone to WhatsApp or Drive, then restore on the new phone
App opened at a new web addressStarts empty, because storage belongs to the web addressKeep pharmacy.vipulanand.in. Move by backup if it ever changes

Ask the phone to keep the data

Today’s app already asks with navigator.storage.persist() at start. v2 keeps that, asks again after installation, and shows the answer in plain words in Backup: “Protected from automatic clean-up”, or “Not protected yet. Install the app and keep backups.” Chrome usually says yes to an installed app. On an iPhone, installing is the protection.

Automatic backups (as automatic as a server-free app can be)

  • Honest limit: a web app can’t run a backup by itself while it’s closed, so v2 nudges at the right moments
  • Backup dot on Home: green if backed up today, amber after 2 to 6 days, red after 7 or more
  • End-of-day nudge: after the last bill of the day, if anything changed since the last backup
  • One tap, Send backup: the phone’s share sheet opens with the file ready for WhatsApp to yourself or Drive. Download is the fallback
  • The file is named with the date, for example shop-backup-2026-11-19.json. It includes the logo and settings, never the PIN itself, only its hash

Restore and verify

  1. Pick the file The app checks it’s this app’s backup and which version wrote it.
  2. Show what’s inside Shop name, date, counts of customers, bills and medicines, the last bill number and the total pending.
  3. Snapshot first The current books are saved as a backup before anything is replaced.
  4. Restore in one go One storage transaction, as today: either all of it goes in or none of it does.
  5. Verify Recount and re-add the totals and compare with the file. Show ‘Restored and checked’, or exactly what differs.

Versions and migrations

  • Add a schemaVersion to the shop settings. Today there’s only a v2: true flag and the database is at version 1
  • Keep the database name counter-khata even after the rename, so existing books open as they are
  • v1 to v2 migration: add an audit store, bill revisions, an archived flag for customers, split letterhead into licence number and GSTIN, and add theme fields
  • Owed before any merge: a backup-compatibility test. Make example-data backups on v1.0 (vipul) and v1.1 (v1.1-footing), restore each into v2, and check every balance, bill total and stock count matches

Saving rules for money

  • Atomic saves: a bill, its stock changes, its payment and the bill number go into one transaction. Today’s commit() already does this; keep it
  • Double-tap lock on every Save: the button greys out until the save is done
  • Bill numbers never repeat and never gap: taken inside the same transaction, voided bills keep their number, nothing is deleted, and a restore can’t silently move numbers backwards
  • Money is kept in paise as whole numbers, as today, so there are no ₹0.01 drift errors
  • The discount limit needs the PIN, as today
  • Audit trail: every edit, void, return, price change and restore is logged and never changed. Returns and voids never delete history
Line amount = MRP × units soldunits per pack − line discount

Example data: MRP ₹45.00 for a strip of 10, selling 15 tablets, gives ₹67.50. A 10% discount takes off ₹6.75, leaving ₹60.75. The bill total rounds to the nearest rupee, ₹61.00, and the bill shows the round-off of +₹0.25.

#GapSeverityProposed fix
1The app links an install file (manifest.webmanifest) and registers an offline file (sw.js), but neither is in the repo. Both return 404 on the live site. So the app doesn’t open without internet, can’t install properly, and on an iPhone isn’t protected from the 7-day wipeCriticalAdd the two small files. 11. Build plan in small milestones

Each milestone goes to the preview only. You try it on your phone, then I move to the next one. Status as of 19 Nov 2026: none started.

M0. Safety fixes and the owed check
  • Popup Save buttons can be tapped at 390×844 and 360×740
  • A double tap on every Save makes exactly one record
  • App opens in airplane mode after the first visit
  • Installs on Android Chrome and an iPhone home screen
  • v1.0 and v1.1 example backups restore with identical totals
M1. Data layer v2
  • v1 books migrate to v2 with no change to any balance
  • Bill edits keep the old version in the audit trail
  • Archiving a customer keeps all their bills and numbers
  • A second tab is blocked from saving
M2. Backup and restore v2
  • Send backup opens WhatsApp and Drive through the share sheet
  • Backup dot turns green, amber and red at the right ages
  • Restore makes a snapshot first and verifies the counts and totals
  • Logo and theme come back after a restore on a fresh browser
M3. First run
  • Five welcome cards swipe, and Skip works
  • Create your shop refuses to finish without the required fields and a PIN
  • Example data is clearly marked and clears in one tap
  • iPhone users are asked to install before creating the shop
M4. Home and new bill
  • A regular-customer bill takes 4 taps
  • Adding a new customer in place keeps the cart
  • Totals and round-off match the worked example
  • Nothing covers a Save button on either phone size
M5. Payment, khata, today’s bills, return and void
  • A payment updates the pending on Home and in the khata
  • A return puts stock back and refunds the charged price
  • A void keeps the bill, crossed out, with its number and reason
M6. Appearance and branding
  • All 6 presets and a custom colour read clearly in light and dark mode
  • Logo shows in the top bar, bill view, PDF and WhatsApp image
  • A 5 MB photo is shrunk under 100 KB
M7. Reports, rename, final review, then live
  • Report totals match the sum of the bills for example data
  • New name everywhere on screen; the books open unchanged
  • Full gap review again on the preview
