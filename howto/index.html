Help for the counter

Counter Khata is a day book for your pharmacy counter. It makes a clean, itemized bill with the discount on every line. It keeps each client’s khata (credit book) and sends bills on WhatsApp.

Everything is saved only on this phone, in this browser. Nothing goes to any server. So please keep backups.

Find your task in the list below. Each one has short numbered steps. If something goes wrong, see When you’re stuck.

Words in boxes are buttons, written exactly as they appear in the app. Words in green are the tabs in the bottom bar: Counter, Clients, Stock, Reports, Shop and Help. Tap a dotted word to see what it stands for.

1. First-time setup

The first time you open the app, it shows a sample shop so you can practise.

  1. Open the app in Chrome from its link, https://pharmacy.vipulanand.in. Do not use private (incognito) mode.
  2. Tap Shop.
  3. Type your Shop name. This is the only field you must fill.
  4. Fill the rest if you like: Pharmacist name, Shop phone, Counter person’s name, Address, and Letterhead line (drug licence no., GSTIN).
  5. To add a logo, tap the Logo file box and pick a picture.
  6. Tap Save shop details. Your shop name now prints on every bill.
  7. The app now asks whether to clear the practice books. See Practice mode.
  8. Change the sample PIN 2580 to your own: Change PIN (see PIN).
Tip: The low-stock alert number on the Shop screen is how many packs count as “low”. The default is 2.

2. Practice mode and clearing sample data

Until you save a real shop name, the app is in practice mode. The title shows “(practice)”. The sample clients are Suresh Kumar, Harpreet Kaur, Kamla Devi and Rajesh Sharma, with sample stock and bills. Anything you type in practice mode is marked as practice.

Clear the sample data

  1. Save your shop name in Shop. A box called Clear practice books opens.
  2. If you only practised, keep the tick on “Also remove the … entries typed while practising”.
  3. If you already typed real clients or stock before saving the shop name, untick it so they stay.
  4. Tap Clear practice books. Or tap Not now to do it later.

Later, you can clear it from the yellow banner (Clear them) or from Shop → Clear practice data. These ask for the PIN.

Note: A sample client or medicine that is used in one of your real bills is kept, so your real bill stays correct.

3. Add a client

  1. Tap Clients, then + New client. (On Counter you can also tap + New client.)
  2. Type the Name. Phone is optional but needed for WhatsApp. Use 10 digits.
  3. Coming from a paper book? Type the Opening balance (₹) and the As on date. Use a minus sign for an advance.
  4. Tap Save client.
Do not count twice: If you enter an opening balance, do not also enter the old bills from before that date.

To change details later, open the client and tap Edit client. Changing the opening balance asks for the PIN.

4. Add medicines to stock

A bill can only use medicines that are in the stock list.

  1. Tap Stock, then + Add medicine.
  2. Fill Name, and Strength / size if any (for example 500 mg).
  3. Pick the Type (Tablet, Syrup, Injection, Inhaler and so on). The pack and unit fill in by themselves.
  4. Check Units in one pack (for example 15 tablets in one strip) and Sell loose?
  5. Type the MRP per pack and the Usual discount %.
  6. Type how many packs are on the shelf now, and the nearest Expiry.
  7. Tap Save.

Change stock later

  • On the Stock screen, tap any value to change it. It saves when you tap away. Enter also saves, Esc cancels.
  • For shelf count, type packs, or packs+loose like 12+5.
  • For new stock received, or to hide a medicine, tap ⋯. Then use New stock received, or Hide from list / Delete.
Badges: low means below the low-stock alert, out means none on the shelf, exp means it expires within 60 days.

5. Make an itemized bill with discount

  1. Tap Counter. Under Who is buying? type the client’s name or phone, and tap the client.
  2. Add medicines in any of these ways:
    • Tap a green chip such as + Metformin SR 500 mg ×4 (usual medicines).
    • Tap Repeat last bill to copy the last bill at today’s prices.
    • Search under Add medicine and tap the medicine.
  3. On each line, set the number of packs with − and +. Add Loose units if you sell part of a strip.
  4. Check the Discount % on each line. It starts at the medicine’s usual discount (or the client’s prescription discount). Change it if needed.
  5. Check the totals: MRP total, Discount, Round off, Bill total and Net savings on this bill.
  6. Under Paid by, pick Cash, UPI or Credit.
    • Cash or UPI: leave Received now blank if the full bill is paid. Type the amount if only part is paid. The rest goes on the khata.
    • Credit: the whole bill goes on the khata.
  7. Entering an old paper bill? Change the Bill date. It cannot be a future date.
  8. Check Khata after this bill, then tap Save, or Save & WhatsApp to send it at once.

Charge a different amount

Tap ✎ Change next to the Bill total. Type the amount under Charge this amount instead (₹). It cannot be more than the MRP total. Tap Use calculated to go back.

How the maths works: each line is MRP for the quantity, minus that line’s discount %. The lines are added, then the bill is rounded to the nearest rupee.
Red message on a line? “Shelf shows only …” means stock looks short. You can still save, but please recount the shelf.

6. Walk-in cash sale

  1. On Counter, tap Walk-in cash sale.
  2. Add medicines as in step 5.
  3. Pick Cash or UPI. Type the money handed over in Received now to see Return change.
  4. Tap Save.

A walk-in sale must be paid in full. It cannot go on credit. To give credit, pick or add a client instead.

7. Send the bill on WhatsApp, or print it

  1. Tap Save & WhatsApp when saving. For an old bill, open it (tap it in Today at the counter or in the client’s khata) and tap Send itemized PDF.
  2. The Send to client box shows the bill picture. Wait for “✓ Itemized PDF ready”.
  3. Tap Send PDF on WhatsApp. Choose WhatsApp, then the client. The PDF and the itemized message go together.

If the phone cannot pass the file

A yellow box Two taps instead appears:

  1. Tap ① Save PDF. It goes to Downloads.
  2. Tap ② Open WhatsApp chat. The itemized message is already typed.
  3. In WhatsApp tap 📎 → Document, and pick the saved PDF.

Other buttons in the same box

  • Send picture sends the bill as a picture.
  • Download PDF and Open PDF save or show the PDF.
  • Copy itemized text copies the message, to paste anywhere.
  • Print prints the bill picture on a connected printer.
  • See the WhatsApp message shows the exact text the client gets.

8. Record a payment

Use this when a client pays money towards the khata, without a new bill.

  1. Tap Clients and tap the client.
  2. Tap Amount received.
  3. Type the Amount (₹). Pick Cash or UPI.
  4. Change the Date if it was paid earlier. Add a Note if you like.
  5. Check Pending after, then tap Save payment.

The payment goes against the running balance, not against one medicine. Money paid more than the balance shows as an advance.

Wrong payment? Tap it in the khata and tap Delete payment (needs the PIN). Then enter it again correctly. A payment taken with a bill is changed from the bill instead.

9. Fix a saved bill

Open the bill (tap it in Today at the counter, or in the client’s khata). Pick what you need. Each one asks for the pharmacist PIN.

10. Find a client’s balance

  1. Tap Clients. The list shows everyone, highest pending first, with the total pending at the top.
  2. Type a name or phone in the search box to find one client.
  3. Tap the client. The big number on the right is Pending (red) or Advance (green).
  4. Below it, Khata · newest first lists every bill, return and payment with the running balance. Tap a line to open it.

On Counter, after you pick a client, their Pending khata shows at the top right. Tap it to open the khata. Tap ← All clients to go back to the list.

11. Send a month bill

  1. Open the client and tap Month bill.
  2. Pick the Month. In the first 10 days of a month, it starts at last month.
  3. Check: Brought forward, + Medicines this month, − Returns, − Money received, and Closing due.
  4. Keep Itemized ticked to show every medicine with quantity and discount. Untick for a short statement.
  5. Tap Send statement PDF. Sending works as in step 7.

The khata is never wiped at month end. This month’s closing is next month’s opening, by itself.

12. Prescriptions and refill reminders

  1. Open the client and tap + Add prescription.
  2. Pick the Medicine. Type the Dose and Every (day, week or month).
  3. Type the usual packs per visit. Optional: this client’s discount %, pack size for this client, doctor, start date.
  4. Tap Save.

The app works out the next refill date from what the client actually bought and the dose. Refills due in the next 3 days show on Counter under Refills due. Tap one to start that bill. ⏰ on a chip means a refill is near.

To stop one, open it and untick Still taking this medicine.

13. Reports

Tap Reports. Use ‹ and › or the month box to pick a month. You see:

  • Billed, Collected, Still due, and number of bills with the average.
  • A six-month chart of billed and collected.
  • Top medicines, Highest pending today, Low stock, and medicines expiring within 60 days.
  • Excel buttons: Month statements (all clients) and This month’s bills.

14. Import and export with Excel (CSV)

Export

Tap Shop, then under Excel (CSV) tap one: Khata entries, All bills with items, Clients + balance or Stock. The file downloads and opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

Import clients or stock

  1. Tap Clients template or Stock template to download a sample sheet with the right columns.
  2. Fill it in Excel or Google Sheets. Keep the first row (column names) as it is.
  3. Save or download it as CSV.
  4. Tap Import clients or Import stock and pick the file.
  5. A box shows how many were added, updated or skipped, and why.
Good to know: A client with a phone number already in the app is skipped. A stock row with the same name and strength as an existing medicine updates it, and the shelf count becomes the number in the sheet (it is not added on top).

15. Back up and restore

If this phone is lost or the browser is cleared, the khata goes with it. A backup file is the only way to get it back.

Back up (once a week)

  1. Tap Shop, then Download full backup. (Or tap Back up on the yellow banner.)
  2. A file named like counter-khata-….json goes to Downloads.
  3. Send it to yourself on WhatsApp, or save it to Google Drive.

The app shows a yellow reminder if you have real entries and no backup in the last 7 days.

Restore, or move to a new phone

  1. Put the backup file on the phone.
  2. Open the app, tap Shop, then Restore a backup, and pick the file.
  3. Type the PIN, then tap Replace everything.
Careful: Restore replaces everything on this phone with the backup. It cannot be undone. On a new phone the PIN is the sample 2580 until the backup is restored.

16. PIN and discount limit

The pharmacist PIN protects changes to money: changing a bill amount, editing, returns, voids, deleting a payment, changing an opening balance, clearing practice data and restoring. Once typed, it stays unlocked for 5 minutes.

Change the PIN

  1. Tap Shop → Change PIN. Type the current PIN (sample: 2580).
  2. Type a new PIN of 4 to 8 digits, twice. Tap Save PIN.

Set a discount limit

  1. Tap Shop → Set a limit (or Change or remove limit).
  2. Type the highest discount % allowed at the counter. Leave blank or 0 for no limit.
  3. Tap Save.

With a limit, a line discount cannot go above it. A changed bill amount that cuts deeper than the limit asks for the PIN.

Forgot the PIN? The app has no PIN reset. Keep it written somewhere safe.

17. When you’re stuck

All my bills and clients have disappeared. What happened?

Counter Khata keeps everything only in this browser on this phone. You will see an empty or sample khata if:

  • browser data or site data was cleared, or a cleaner app ran,
  • you opened it in private (incognito) mode,
  • you opened it in another browser, or on another phone,
  • the phone ran low on space and the browser cleaned up.

Fix: open it again in the same browser you always use. If the data is really gone, restore your latest backup: Shop → Restore a backup. See Back up and restore.

At the bottom of Shop the app says whether storage is protected from automatic clean-up.

WhatsApp does not open, or the PDF does not attach.
  • Open the app from its https link in Chrome, not as a saved file. A file copy cannot pass files to WhatsApp.
  • Use the Two taps instead box: ① Save PDF, then ② Open WhatsApp chat, then attach the PDF from Downloads.
  • If WhatsApp opens without the client’s chat, the client’s phone is missing or is not 10 digits. Fix it with Edit client.
  • If the PDF says it could not be made, use Send picture or Copy itemized text.
The total or the discount looks wrong.
  • Check Units in one pack on the line. MRP is per pack, so a wrong pack size changes the price of loose units.
  • Check the Discount % on each line. It starts at the medicine’s usual discount, or the client’s prescription discount.
  • If a discount limit is set, a higher discount is cut down to the limit.
  • The Bill total is rounded to the nearest rupee. The Round off line shows the difference.
  • If someone used ✎ Change, you will see “Changed by pharmacist”. Tap Use calculated to undo it before saving.
  • For wrong MRP or discount in stock, fix it on the Stock screen. Old bills keep the price they were saved with.
I saved a bill with a mistake. How do I correct it?

Open the bill and use ✎ Change amount, Edit items, Return packs or Void. See Fix a saved bill. A bill is never deleted. A voided bill stays, crossed out, so the record is honest.

I entered a wrong payment.

Open the client, tap the payment line in the khata, tap Delete payment and type the PIN. Then use Amount received again. If it came with a bill, fix it with Edit items on that bill.

Which entries are practice and which are real?

Anything typed before you save a shop name in Shop is practice. The title says “(practice)” while you are in practice mode. After you save the shop name, new entries are real.

When you clear practice data, untick Also remove the … entries typed while practising if some of those were real. See Practice mode.

Does it work without internet?

Bills, khata, stock and backups are all saved on the phone, so they do not need internet once the app is open. Sending on WhatsApp needs internet.

Right now, opening the app page itself may need internet. Keep the tab open in Chrome during the day. Full “install to home screen” and offline opening are not ready yet.

Can I print a bill?

Yes. Open the bill, tap Send itemized PDF, and in the box tap Print. Or tap Download PDF and print the PDF. A month statement prints the same way from Month bill → Send statement PDF.

It asks for a PIN. What is it?

It is the pharmacist PIN. On a new or practice setup it is 2580. Change it under Shop → Change PIN. See PIN and discount limit.

A medicine is not found when I search on Counter.

It is not in the stock list, or it was hidden. Add it in Stock with + Add medicine. You can search by name or by salt.

Stock shows below zero, or “Shelf went below zero”.

More was sold than the shelf count in the app. Count the shelf, then on Stock tap On shelf for that medicine and type the real count (packs, or packs+loose like 12+5).

“Walk-in sale must be paid in full”.

A walk-in customer has no khata, so the sale cannot go on credit. Tap Change and pick the client, or add them with + New client.

“Bill date cannot be in the future”, or a date problem.

The app uses the phone’s date. Check the phone’s date and time settings. Old paper bills can be entered with an earlier Bill date.

I tapped Help and the browser asked “Leave site?”.

A bill was still open on Counter and not saved. Tap Cancel, save the bill first, then open Help.

“Could not open the khata storage”.

Close other tabs of this app, do not use private mode, and open it again in Chrome.
